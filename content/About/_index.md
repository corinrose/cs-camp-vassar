Welcome to CS Camp!  The camp is possible through a generous grant from the Technology Fund and we're delighted that you are spending your summer with us.  

We'll be meeting for six weeks M-F starting 9:30am with camp running till about 5pm, with a break for lunch from 12-1pm.  We'll have a class at 10am which introduces the topic for the day.  You'll then work on exercises with help from our two coaches, Corin and Ian.  They will be in the lab from 9:30 to 6pm to answer any of your questions.  You can go to the [schedule](http://cscamp.cs.vassar.edu) from any page by clicking on the campfire logo in the top-left corner of the page.

In addition to the exercises in class, you will work on a larger project, one of your own choosing!  The last day of class we'll gather for a Symposium where you can present your work to the class.  Enjoy!

Textbook
--------

We'll be using the [CS for All](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/csforall/) textbook.  Links to the reading for day are posted on the class schedule.  There is also a [pdf version of the texbook](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~cs5grad/cs5book.pdf) which you can download and read instead of the online version.
