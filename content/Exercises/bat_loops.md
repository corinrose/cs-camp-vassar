Objective
---------
 - Practice, practice, practice!

For this exercise, you'll be writing code on CodingBat. Complete any 5 of the following 12 problems about [lists](http://codingbat.com/python/List-2) and [strings](http://codingbat.com/python/String-2).

As a bonus, feel free to go on and solve the rest of the problems linked to above. If you're so inclined, you can use [CodingBat](http://codingbat.com/python) as a resource for practice questions.
